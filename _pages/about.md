Hello, I'm Samarth Jain — a Staff Software Engineer & Tech Lead with 12+ years of experience designing, architecting, and building scalable, distributed backend systems.

My engineering interests lie at the intersection of distributed systems, backend architecture, data-intensive applications, and large-scale system design. I enjoy tackling complex engineering challenges, understanding the underlying principles of distributed computing, and making informed architectural decisions that balance scalability, reliability, performance, and operational complexity.

This blog serves as a collection of technical deep dives, engineering explorations, and practical insights drawn from my experience building backend systems. I'll be covering topics ranging from distributed consensus, transaction processing, concurrency, and fault tolerance to database internals, API design, system architecture, and performance engineering.

My goal is to go beyond surface-level explanations and explore the fundamentals, trade-offs, failure scenarios, and design considerations that shape production-grade systems.

If you're interested in understanding how complex backend systems work, why certain architectural decisions are made, and how to engineer systems that scale reliably, you're in the right place.
